**Reservations** lists every booking from every channel. Tabs: **Upcoming** (including guests staying now), **Past** and **Cancelled**. Search by guest, booking code or property. Click a row to open the booking.

## A booking's page

It shows the stay, the guest and **Notes for the team**, with what the channel sent beside them, and a link to see it on the Calendar.

You can change:

- the guest's name, email and phone
- **Times for this stay**: check-in from and check-out by, if they differ from the home's usual times
- notes for the team, which guests and channels never see

The dates, party size and price of a channel booking can't be changed here. Change them on the channel; the channel tells ViVi OS. If a channel later sends new guest details they replace yours, but a detail it doesn't send (Airbnb shares no email address) keeps what you typed.

## Making a booking

**New booking** (or **New → Booking**) is for direct or repeat guests and phone bookings: property, dates, guest name and contact, adults and children, an arrival time, a price and notes. It becomes a confirmed direct booking that holds the nights. It is refused if a booking or block already holds them.

## Money on a booking

The booking shows:

- **Total**: what the channel or the booking says.
- Extra charges the team adds, such as a late check-out or a pet. A minus makes it a discount.
- Payments received: bank transfer, cash, card taken elsewhere, or collected by the channel. A minus is a refund. When recording a refund, say whether it was for the stay or the cleaning; this matters for the owner's statement.
- The balance: **Balance due** or **Paid in full**.

Use **Add a charge**, **Record a payment** and **Record a refund**. Admins, operations and accounts can do this. Recording a payment here does not charge anyone. To take a card payment or hold a deposit, see [Payments and deposits](/guides/payments-and-deposits/).

## Cancelling

- **A direct booking**: **Cancel booking**, give a reason, then confirm. The nights are free at once. ViVi OS keeps the reason and who cancelled it.
- **A channel booking**: cancel it on the channel. The channel tells ViVi OS and the nights are freed. ViVi OS won't let you cancel it here, so the two never disagree.

A cancellation also removes the open clean for that stay and takes the door code off the lock.

## The guest's page

Every confirmed booking has a private link to **The guest's page**. On the booking:

- **Copy link** copies it to send to the guest.
- **See what the guest sees** opens it.

The automatic messages already include the link, and the Inbox has a **Stay link** button. See [Guest stay page](/guides/guest-stay-page/).

## Other actions

- **Message guest** opens the booking's conversation in the [Inbox](/guides/inbox/).
- **Door code** shows the stay's code once the lock holds it, for roles that may see codes.
- **Damage claim** starts a [claim](/guides/claims/) for this stay.

The field team can't see bookings, because bookings carry guests' contact details.
