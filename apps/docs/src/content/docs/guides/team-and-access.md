Your team is managed on **Workspace → Team**. Only admins can invite people, change roles and limit access.

## Inviting someone

**Invite a colleague**: their name, email, **Role**, **Homes they can see**, and **Jobs they do** (for the field team). Then **Send invitation**.

They get an email with a link and sign in with it; there are no passwords. An invitation stays open for 14 days and shows as "Invited, not signed in yet" until it is used. **Cancel invitation** withdraws it.

A sign-in lasts 48 hours. After that, people sign in again.

To add cleaners and maintenance staff from Guesty without sending them an email, see [Tasks and the field team](/guides/tasks-and-field-team/#adding-the-field-team-from-guesty).

## Roles

| Role | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| **Operator admin** | Everything, including the team, channels, custom fields, the booking website and the workspace. |
| **Operations** | Run the homes: calendar, bookings, guests, tasks, locks, claims, owners, expenses, insights. |
| **Guest comms** | Talk to guests: bookings and the Inbox. Can read claims. |
| **Accounts** | Money: payments on bookings, owners and statements, who is paid, expenses, claims, insights. Read the rest. |
| **Field team** | Cleaners and maintenance. See only **My jobs** on their phone. |

Some details are limited further:

- **Lock codes, the Wi-Fi password and other secrets** are seen by admins, operations and guest comms only. Never by accounts or V-01 support. The field team sees only what is marked "Field team sees it", inside a job, on its day.
- **Who is paid for each home**, with bank details, is seen by admins and accounts only.
- The field team doesn't see bookings, guests' contact details, money or claims.

**Owners** are not on your team. They have their own log-in, given from the owner's page. See [Owners and statements](/guides/owners-and-statements/#the-owner-log-in).

## Changing and removing

An admin can change a person's role, name, jobs and homes, and **Remove** them. Nobody can change or remove their own membership, and the last admin can't be removed.

## Access by home

An admin can limit anyone except another admin to chosen homes: **Change homes** on the team list, or **Homes they can see** in the invitation. Someone limited sees only those homes on every page: Calendar, bookings, guests, Inbox, tasks, claims, expenses, owners, statements, Insights and Today. Admins always see every home.

## Your own profile

Your name in the top bar opens **My profile**: your name, email, when you signed in, and your companies. Set your name so the team sees it instead of your email, on tasks, in the Inbox and on photos.

## Support access by V-01 staff

V-01 staff can't see inside your workspace on their own. To help you, they open **support access**:

- They must give a reason you can read.
- It is read-only: they can't change anything.
- It lasts from 5 minutes to 4 hours, then closes by itself.
- They never see lock codes or other secrets.

Every visit (who, why, when and for how long) is listed under **V-01 support visits** at the bottom of the Workspace page.
