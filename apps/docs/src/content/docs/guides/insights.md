**Insights** shows how your homes are doing: occupancy, nightly rate and revenue, compared with the period before. Admins, operations, accounts and ViVi OS support can see it.

## Choosing a period

Pick a **Period**: **This month**, **Last month**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** or **This year**. Each figure is compared with the same length of time just before it. For example, last 30 days against the 30 days before that.

## The period at a glance

- **Occupancy**: booked nights out of available nights. Blocked nights don't count as available.
- **Nightly rate**: the average price of a booked night.
- **Revenue**
- **Per available night**: revenue divided by every night that could have been booked.
- **Arrivals**
- **Average stay**
- **Expenses**: going up shows as bad.
- **Damage claimed** and received.

## Month by month

Two charts for the twelve months to the end of the period: **Revenue, month by month** and **Occupancy, month by month**. They are kept as two charts, so each has one clear scale.

## Side by side

Every home, owner or area in one table. Choose with **Group by**: **Homes**, **Owners** or **Areas**. Click a column heading to sort by it.

## Channels

Each channel's share of revenue, its nightly rate, and how far ahead its guests book.

## How the money is counted

Each confirmed booking's money is spread over its nights. A stay that crosses two months counts partly in each. Today and the owner statements count the same way, so all three agree.

If you can see only some homes, Insights shows only those homes.
