---
title: Tasks and the field team
description: Cleans, maintenance and inspections, who gets which job, and what cleaners see on their phones.
---

**Tasks** are the jobs at your homes: cleans, maintenance and inspections. Each is on a home, due on a day and by a time, assigned to a person, with a checklist, notes and photos.

## Cleans are made for you

V-01 makes a clean for every check-out. It is due by the next guest's arrival if someone arrives that day, and goes to the home's usual cleaner with your cleaning checklist. If the booking is cancelled, its open clean goes too. When a clean is marked done, the home is marked clean.

The usual cleaner and the usual maintenance person are set on each home's **Settings**. Maintenance jobs made by the office, and damage the cleaners report, go to the usual maintenance person.

## For the office

The Tasks page has **Today**, **Next two weeks**, **All open** and **Done**, and can be filtered by property and person.

- **New task** (or **New → Task**): choose the property, what kind, title, day, by what time, who it is assigned to, and notes. Leave **Assigned to** as "Nobody yet" and anyone on the crew who can do it may take it.
- **Task settings**: what each kind of task starts with (title and checklist), and whether a clean is made at every check-out.
- On a repair, record **What it cost** and **Save cost**. When the job is done the cost is logged as an expense on the home.

Admins and operations make and assign tasks. Everyone in the office can see them.

## Skills: who can take which job

Each person on the team has **Jobs they do**: **Cleaning**, **Maintenance**, both or neither.

- A clean can go only to someone with Cleaning who works on that home.
- A repair can go only to someone with Maintenance who works on that home.
- An inspection can go to anyone.

The same rule decides who can be a home's usual cleaner or usual maintenance person. Set skills on **Workspace → Team**.

## For cleaners and maintenance staff

The field team signs in to a simple phone screen: their name, **My jobs** and Sign out. Nothing else. They see their own jobs and any unassigned job they can take. Starting an unassigned job makes it theirs.

On a job they can:

- **Start**, tick off the checklist, and **Mark done**
- add photos with the phone's camera. A clean asks for **Before** and **After** photos, and **Something found** for anything else.
- see the **Getting in** box, with the address, a link to Maps, and any detail marked "Field team sees it" (in the standard set, the bottom lock code). It shows only on the job's day, while the job is open.
- **Report damage**: this makes a maintenance task on the same home for the office.

Every photo has the time, the home and its address, and who took it written onto the picture, like the Timemark app. The time comes from the camera. A photo taken more than an hour before it was added says so ("Taken 14:50 · added 17:51"), so an old photo can't pass as new. Photos are private.

## Adding the field team from Guesty

On **Workspace → Team**, **From Guesty** lists the people on your Guesty account, grouped as cleaners, maintenance and everyone else. Their Guesty tags give their skills, and their Guesty homes give the homes they work on.

Tick who to add and press **Add to the team**. Nobody is sent an email. They join the field team, can be given jobs straight away, and sign in later with their email as the same person. Someone with no email in Guesty can't be added this way; invite them instead.
