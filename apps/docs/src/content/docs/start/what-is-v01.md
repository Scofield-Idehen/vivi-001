title: What V-01 is
description: What V-01 does, who it is for, its parts, and how it sits beside Guesty and Channex.
---

V-01 is a property management system for short-term rental operators: companies that run holiday homes for their owners and let them on Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo and their own website. It replaces Guesty. Vivistays is the first operator to use it.

V-01 keeps one copy of everything about your homes: the listing, the calendar, the bookings, the guests, the jobs for the cleaners, the money and the owners. Your team works from that one copy, and V-01 keeps the channels in step with it.

## Who it is for

- **The office team**: people who take bookings, talk to guests, plan cleans and repairs, and look after owners.
- **The field team**: cleaners and maintenance staff, who see only their own jobs on a phone.
- **Owners** of the homes, who can log in to see their homes' bookings and their statements.
- **Guests**, who never sign in. They get a private page for their stay, and can book on your website.

## The parts

- **The staff app.** Where your team works: [Today](/guides/today/), [Calendar](/guides/calendar/), [Reservations](/guides/reservations/), [Inbox](/guides/inbox/), [Tasks](/guides/tasks-and-field-team/), [Properties](/guides/properties/), [Owners](/guides/owners-and-statements/), Channels & sync, [Claims](/guides/claims/), Expenses, [Insights](/guides/insights/) and Workspace. Each person sees what their role allows.
- **The guest's stay page.** A private link for each confirmed booking, with check-in details, the door code on the right days, Wi-Fi and house rules. See [Guest stay page](/guides/guest-stay-page/).
- **The booking website.** Your own site where guests search your homes, pick dates and pay by card. See [Booking website](/guides/booking-website/).
- **The owner log-in.** A simple page where an owner sees their homes' booked nights and their finalised statements.
- **The V-01 console.** Used only by V-01 staff to set up operators and check that everything is syncing. V-01 staff cannot see inside your workspace unless they open support access, which you can see. See [Team and access](/guides/team-and-access/).

## Guesty and Channex

**Channex** is the channel manager. It sits between V-01 and Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo. V-01 sends it prices, available nights and stay rules; Channex sends bookings back. Your existing listings are linked as they are, so they keep their reviews, photos and ranking. Channex does not carry photos, descriptions or amenities, so you change those on each channel, using the copy buttons on the property page.

**Guesty** keeps running your homes while you move. You can bring your homes, bookings, owners, staff and guest conversations across from Guesty. A home brought from Guesty stays run by Guesty until it is moved over: V-01 sends nothing to the channels for it. A home is never run by both at once, because two managers on one listing can sell the same night twice. See [Channels](/guides/channels/).

## Where things stand

V-01 runs on staging while Vivistays moves over from Guesty. Everything in these guides is built, but a few
connections aren't switched on for real yet: the live Channex connection to Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo,
emails sent by V-01, and payments in live (not test) mode. Until then, Guesty keeps running the channels for
Vivistays' homes.
