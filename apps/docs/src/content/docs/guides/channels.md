---
title: Channels
description: Connecting Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo through Channex, and moving homes over from Guesty.
---

V-01 reaches Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo through **Channex**, a channel manager. V-01 sends Channex each home's prices, free nights and stay rules; Channex passes them to the channels and sends bookings back. Channex links to your **existing** listings, so they keep their reviews, photos and ranking.

Channex does not carry photos, descriptions or amenities. Change those on each channel, using the copy buttons on the property page. A brand-new listing is made on the channel first, then linked.

## Guesty and V-01 never run the same home

A home brought from Guesty stays **run by Guesty** until it is moved over. V-01 sends nothing to the channels for it. Two managers on one listing can sell the same night twice, so a home is never run by both. A home added in V-01 starts as **not connected** and sends nothing either.

Moving a home over is done by V-01 staff, once its listings are off Guesty. Each home's Settings shows **Channels run by**.

## 1. Add your channel accounts (admin)

On **Channels & sync**, **Add an account** for each sign-in you use on a channel, for example "Airbnb A", "Airbnb B", "Booking.com A". Note who signs in and who holds the 2-step code. Then **Connect**.

- **Airbnb and Vrbo** give a link. Whoever signs in to that account opens it and approves Channex. **Open the link**, or copy it and send it to them.
- **Booking.com** needs the **Booking.com hotel id**, found in the extranet, top left, beside the property name. Booking.com approves it on their side.

Once connected, V-01 lists the listings Channex sees on the account, refreshed hourly.

A Vrbo account that was connected to Guesty needs Guesty's connection taken off first; Vrbo support may need to help, and any iCal links must be removed.

## 2. Put each home in Channex (admin)

On a home's page, **Connect to Channex**. Or, on **Properties**, **Connect all to Channex** does every home not yet there. The Properties list shows each one as **In Channex**, **Connecting**, **Not yet** or **Couldn't connect**; **Try again** retries one that failed.

Connecting sends nothing to any channel.

## 3. Link each listing

On a home's page, each channel has a card. On an unlinked card, **Link a listing** and pick one of the connected accounts' free listings. A linked card names the listing, opens it on the channel's site, and can be unlinked. One listing per channel per home.

For Airbnb, V-01 also sends the home's fees and booking rules: nightly and weekend price, guests included and extra-guest fee, cleaning fee, security deposit, instant book, check-in and check-out times, and the cancellation policy. **Pause on Airbnb** takes the listing off Airbnb without unlinking it, like Guesty's "temporarily unlist"; **Resume on Airbnb** puts it back. Booking.com and Vrbo listings are paused on the channel itself.

## Keeping it in step

- Channex closes a booked night by itself the moment a booking arrives, so a home is not sold twice even if V-01 is briefly behind. V-01 checks this setting daily and turns it back on if someone switched it off.
- Every night, V-01 compares the next 90 nights with what Channex holds and resends anything that differs. The home's page says when it was last checked.
- **Channels & sync** shows changes waiting to go out, changes that failed, the last booking in, and the last check.

Problems show on [Today](/guides/today/) under **Needs attention**.
