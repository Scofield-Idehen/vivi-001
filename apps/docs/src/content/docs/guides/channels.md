---
title: Channels
description: Connecting Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo, and moving homes over from your previous system.
---

ViVi OS connects to Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo through its **channel sync**. It sends each home's prices, free nights and stay rules to the channels, and their bookings come back into ViVi OS. Your **existing** listings are linked as they are, so they keep their reviews, photos and ranking.

Channel sync doesn't carry photos, descriptions or amenities. Change those on each channel, using the copy buttons on the property page. A brand-new listing is made on the channel first, then linked.

## Two systems never run the same home

A home brought across from your previous system stays run by that system until it is moved over. ViVi OS sends nothing to the channels for it. Two systems on one listing can sell the same night twice, so a home is never run by both. A home added in ViVi OS starts as **not connected** and sends nothing either.

Moving a home over is done by ViVi OS staff, once its listings are off the previous system. Each home's Settings shows **Channels run by**.

## 1. Add your channel accounts (admin)

On **Channels & sync**, **Add an account** for each sign-in you use on a channel, for example "Airbnb A", "Airbnb B", "Booking.com A". Note who signs in and who holds the 2-step code. Then **Connect**.

- **Airbnb and Vrbo** give a link. Whoever signs in to that account opens it and approves the connection. **Open the link**, or copy it and send it to them.
- **Booking.com** needs the **Booking.com hotel id**, found in the extranet, top left, beside the property name. Booking.com approves it on their side.

Once connected, ViVi OS lists the listings on the account, refreshed hourly.

A Vrbo account that was connected to another system needs that connection taken off first; Vrbo support may need to help, and any iCal links must be removed.

## 2. Put each home in channel sync (admin)

On a home's page, **Connect to channel sync**. Or, on **Properties**, **Connect all to channel sync** does every home not yet there. The Properties list shows each one as **In channel sync**, **Connecting**, **Not yet** or **Couldn't connect**; **Try again** retries one that failed.

Connecting sends nothing to any channel.

## 3. Link each listing

On a home's page, each channel has a card. On an unlinked card, **Link a listing** and pick one of the connected accounts' free listings. A linked card names the listing, opens it on the channel's site, and can be unlinked. One listing per channel per home.

For Airbnb, ViVi OS also sends the home's fees and booking rules: nightly and weekend price, guests included and extra-guest fee, cleaning fee, security deposit, instant book, check-in and check-out times, and the cancellation policy. **Pause on Airbnb** takes the listing off Airbnb without unlinking it; **Resume on Airbnb** puts it back. Booking.com and Vrbo listings are paused on the channel itself.

## Keeping it in step

- Channel sync closes a booked night by itself the moment a booking arrives, so a home is not sold twice even if ViVi OS is briefly behind. ViVi OS checks this setting daily and turns it back on if someone switched it off.
- Every night, ViVi OS compares the next 90 nights with what channel sync holds and resends anything that differs. The home's page says when it was last checked.
- **Channels & sync** shows changes waiting to go out, changes that failed, the last booking in, and the last check.

Problems show on [Today](/guides/today/) under **Needs attention**.
