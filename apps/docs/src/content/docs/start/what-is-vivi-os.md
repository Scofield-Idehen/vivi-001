title: What ViVi OS is
description: What ViVi OS does, who it is for, its parts, and how it connects to the channels.

ViVi OS is a property management system for short-term rental operators: companies that run holiday homes for their owners and let them on Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo and their own website. Vivistays is the first operator to use it.

ViVi OS keeps one copy of everything about your homes: the listing, the calendar, the bookings, the guests, the jobs for the cleaners, the money and the owners. Your team works from that one copy, and ViVi OS keeps the channels in step with it.

## Who it is for

- **The office team**: people who take bookings, talk to guests, plan cleans and repairs, and look after owners.
- **The field team**: cleaners and maintenance staff, who see only their own jobs on a phone.
- **Owners** of the homes, who can log in to see their homes' bookings and their statements.
- **Guests**, who never sign in. They get a private page for their stay, and can book on your website.

## The parts

- **The staff app.** Where your team works: [Today](/guides/today/), [Calendar](/guides/calendar/), [Reservations](/guides/reservations/), [Inbox](/guides/inbox/), [Tasks](/guides/tasks-and-field-team/), [Properties](/guides/properties/), [Owners](/guides/owners-and-statements/), Channels & sync, [Claims](/guides/claims/), Expenses, [Insights](/guides/insights/) and Workspace. Each person sees what their role allows.
- **The guest's stay page.** A private link for each confirmed booking, with check-in details, the door code on the right days, Wi-Fi and house rules. See [Guest stay page](/guides/guest-stay-page/).
- **The booking website.** Your own site where guests search your homes, pick dates and pay by card. See [Booking website](/guides/booking-website/).
- **The owner log-in.** A simple page where an owner sees their homes' booked nights and their finalised statements.
- **The ViVi OS console.** Used only by ViVi OS staff to set up operators and check that everything is syncing. ViVi OS staff cannot see inside your workspace unless they open support access, which you can see. See [Team and access](/guides/team-and-access/).

## The channels, and moving from your current system

**Channels.** ViVi OS connects to Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo. It sends them prices, available nights and stay rules, and their bookings come back into ViVi OS. Your existing listings are linked as they are, so they keep their reviews, photos and ranking. Photos, descriptions and amenities aren't sent, so you change those on each channel, using the copy buttons on the property page.

**Moving over.** Your current system keeps running your homes while you move. Your homes, bookings, owners, staff and guest conversations can be brought across. A home brought across stays run by your current system until it is moved over: ViVi OS sends nothing to the channels for it. A home is never run by both at once, because two systems on one listing can sell the same night twice. See [Channels](/guides/channels/).

## Where things stand

ViVi OS runs on staging while Vivistays moves over. Everything in these guides is built, but a few
connections aren't switched on for real yet: the live connection to Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo,
emails sent by ViVi OS, and payments in live (not test) mode. Until then, Vivistays' current system keeps
running the channels for its homes.
