title: Claims

A **claim** collects everything you need to ask a channel or a guest to pay for damage after a stay. There is one claim per booking. ViVi OS doesn't file the claim for you: a person files it on the channel or with the guest, and records it in ViVi OS.

## Starting a claim

- From a booking's page: **Damage claim**.
- From **Claims**: **Start a claim**, or pick from **Damage reported, no claim yet**, which lists stays where the team reported damage.
- From **New → Damage claim**.

## What a claim holds

- **What happened**: a short account.
- **What was damaged**: each thing and its cost, with the total claimed.
- **Evidence**:
  - **Before the stay**: photos from the last clean before the guest arrived.
  - **After the stay**: photos from every job at the home after they left, and the team's damage reports.
  - **Added to this claim**: your own photos, videos, receipts and invoices. Up to 40 files, 100 MB each.
- **Spent putting it right**: expenses recorded for this claim, with receipts.

Photos added to a claim get the time, the home and who added it written on the picture. The original is kept untouched beside it, because Airbnb doesn't accept altered images. On a phone, **Take a photo** opens the camera. Receipts, PDFs and videos are kept exactly as uploaded.

**Download evidence** gives one zip file: a written summary, the before and after photos, the originals, and the files.

## The deadline

ViVi OS works out the deadline: 14 days after check-out. For Airbnb it is also before the next guest checks in, whichever comes first. You can change it with **Change the deadline**. Today and the bell warn you three days before. The Claims list is ordered by deadline.

## Where it stands

Under **Where it stands**, move the claim through:

- **Getting ready**
- **Filed**: with the day, who it was filed with, and **Their reference** (the case number they give you)
- **Paid**: with the amount received
- **Closed**: with the outcome

Money received for repairs the owner paid for goes back to the owner on their statement, in the month it arrived. See [Owners and statements](/guides/owners-and-statements/).

## Who can do what

Admins, operations and accounts make and file claims. Guest comms and ViVi OS support can read them. The field team doesn't see claims; they report damage from their jobs.

## Damage protection

Damage protection from a provider is a separate decision and not built. Claims work without it.
