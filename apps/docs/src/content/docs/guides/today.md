**Today** is the first page in the staff app. It shows the state of your homes at a glance and what needs someone now. It starts with the day's check-ins and check-outs and the revenue trend, but it keeps the lists short.

## Daily overview

Choose **Today**, **Tomorrow** or **Next 7 days**. You see:

- **Check-ins** and **Check-outs** as done out of total, for example 3/11. A guest counts as checked in once their check-in time has passed: the time agreed for that stay, or the home's usual time.
- **Turnovers**: homes where one guest leaves and the next arrives the same day, shortest gap first.
- **Staying**: who is in.
- Units that still need cleaning.

## Revenue

Pick a month to compare with the month before: the average daily rate day by day, and total revenue by week. Each confirmed booking's money is spread over its nights, so a stay that crosses two months counts in both. Only roles that can see bookings see this part.

## Trends overview

For the next 7 or 30 days: check-ins, check-outs and turnovers as bars, and occupancy as a shaded area on its own 0–100% scale. Click an item in the legend to hide or show it.

## Your day

Four tabs, each with a count:

- **Needs attention**: things to sort out. For example: two bookings on the same nights, a change a channel refused, a guest waiting for a reply, a claim deadline within three days, a guest arriving today or tomorrow without their deposit held, a door code that didn't reach a lock, a lock battery under 25%, or a channel account waiting to be authorised. Click a row to go where it is fixed.
- **Checking out**
- **Checking in**
- **Cleans due**

Each tab shows four cards. **View all** opens the full list on its own page.

## What each role sees

Everyone sees the lists that apply to them. Money and channel details need a role that can see bookings, so the field team never sees them. The field team does not use Today: they sign in straight to **My jobs**. Someone limited to certain homes sees only those homes here.

## The top bar

On every page:

- **New** makes what you need most often: a booking, task, blocked dates, a message to a guest, an expense, a damage claim, an owner or a property. Press N to open it, then the letter shown.
- The **bell** lists what needs someone.
- Your **name** opens My profile and Sign out.
