The **Inbox** keeps every guest's conversation in one place. There is one conversation per booking, whichever site the guest booked on. The guest keeps using that site's chat and never notices. Your reply goes back where the guest is.

## How replies reach the guest

- **Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo and Expedia guests**: through that site's chat. This connection is not switched on yet. Until it is, what you write waits in the conversation, says so, and is sent the moment it is connected.
- **Direct guests**: by email. Email needs your sending address set up; until then an email waits in the same way.

Texts are not built yet.

Conversations from your previous system's inbox are brought across with the import, for bookings that ended in the last 90 days and every later one.

## The list

Three views: **Needs a reply**, **Open** and **Every booking**. Guests waiting for an answer come first, with how long they have waited. This is the response time the channels judge hosts on: amber within the day, red after it. Today also says how many guests are waiting.

Search by guest, property or booking code. Opening a conversation marks it read. **Done** hides it until the guest writes again.

## Writing a reply

Type in **Your message** and press **Send**, or Ctrl + Enter.

- **Saved replies**: your team's own answers to common questions, such as parking or early arrival. Pick one while writing.
- **Stay link**: adds the link to the guest's [stay page](/guides/guest-stay-page/).

From a booking, **Message guest** opens its conversation. Email, call and text from your own device are still offered there too.

## Automatic messages

Four messages are sent into each booking's conversation when their time comes, in your time zone:

- booking confirmed
- the day before arrival, at 10:00
- the day of arrival, at 09:00 (off until you want it)
- the day before check-out, at 17:00

Open **Automatic messages** to switch each on or off and change its words and time. Messages can fill in details such as the arrival day, check-in time, number of nights, the stay link and the stay's door code, once the smart lock has it. The list of what can be filled in is under **The words a message can fill in**.

An automatic message is not a reply, so the guest still counts as waiting.

## Who can do what

Everyone in the office can read the Inbox. Admins, operations and guest comms can reply and change automatic messages and saved replies. ViVi OS support can only read. The field team doesn't see it.

## Not yet

- Accepting or declining Airbnb booking requests and enquiries. Handle these in the Airbnb app for now.
- Texts.
- AI-suggested replies.
