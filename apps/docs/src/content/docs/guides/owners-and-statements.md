**Owners** lists whose homes you run, how to reach them, what each is due this month so far, and who can log in. Admins, operations and accounts can see and change owners.

## Adding owners

- **From your previous system**: the import brings each owner with all their homes. Owners with several homes come across complete.
- **By hand**: **Add an owner** (or **New → Owner**): name, company, email, phone, **Management fee (%)** and notes.

Give an owner their homes with **Choose homes** on their page, or set **Owner** on a home's Settings. **Homes without an owner** on the Owners page lists homes with no owner yet, each given to an owner with **Give to…**.

## Terms and contracts

On an owner's page, under **Homes and terms**, set each home's deal from a month on:

- **Percentage fee**
- **Fixed monthly rent**
- **No management fee (board)**

Also set who arranges repairs (you, or the owner) and the contract the terms come from. Earlier months keep the terms they had. A home with no terms uses the owner's management fee, and the page asks you to set them.

Under **Contracts**, **Add a contract** uploads the signed management agreement with its signed, start and end dates. Contracts aren't brought across by the import, so these come from your office.

**Who is paid** holds the payee, sort code and account number for each home. Only admins and accounts can see or change it.

## Expenses

**Expenses** records money spent on a home, or on the business: the day, what, kind, who was paid, the amount, a receipt, and who bears it (**charged to the owner**, or the company keeps it). An expense can be linked to a damage claim.

Some costs are logged by themselves: each finished clean logs the home's cleaning cost as your expense, and a repair's cost, when the job is done, is charged to the owner (yours on a fixed-rent home, or nothing when the owner arranges repairs).

## What the owner is due

For each stay, the nights in the month take their share of what the guest paid, less the channel's fee, the cleaning fee (yours, for the cleaner) and stay refunds. What's left is what the stay brought in, and the terms give the owner's part. Expenses charged to the owner come off; claim money received for repairs they paid for comes back, in the month it arrived.

## Statements

A month's statement is a **Draft**, worked out live, until you **Finalise** it. Then its figures are kept as they are; later bookings or expenses don't move them.

- **Mark paid** with the day and the bank reference. **Mark unpaid** undoes it.
- **Reopen** turns an unpaid finalised statement back into a live draft.
- **Print or save PDF** prints the statement alone: the summary, every stay with its nights and money in the month, and the expenses charged.

The **Statements** tab on the Owners page shows every owner for a month (last month by default), what's due, and how far each statement has got.

## The owner log-in

An owner can log in to see their own homes. On the owner's page, under **Owner log-in**, choose **Invite to log in**; or use **Invite owners to log in** on the Owners page for many at once. The owner signs in with the link in their email, with no password.

Signed in, they see:

- **Your homes**: booked and free nights, with no guest names or contacts
- **Earned so far this month**, before the fee and expenses
- their finalised **Statements**, without your notes, to print or save

They see nothing else. Owners aren't on your team list and can't be given tasks. **Remove log-in** ends their access.
