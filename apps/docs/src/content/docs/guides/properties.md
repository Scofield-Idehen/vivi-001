**Properties** lists your homes. Each home has its own page with the whole listing, which you can edit part by part. Admins and operations can change homes; the rest of the office can look.

## Adding a home

**Add property** (or **New → Property**) opens a panel that asks one thing at a time, in Airbnb's order:

1. **About the place**: the kind of place, what guests have (the whole place, a private room or a shared room), the name and address, guests and rooms, check-in and check-out times.
2. **Make it stand out**: amenities, photos, title and description.
3. **Finish up**: pricing, then check and add.

Only the name is required. Your draft is kept in that browser until you add the home. Photos are held until the home exists, then uploaded; a page reload loses them.

Homes can also come across from Guesty. See [Setting up an operator](/start/set-up/).

## The home's page

The top shows the home at a glance: title and address; bedrooms, bathrooms, beds, guests and photos; the channels it is on; clean or dirty; and buttons to its calendar and bookings. **Ready for the channels** lists what the channels still need: five photos, a title, a description, amenities, beds in each room, the full address, how guests get in, and a nightly price.

Under **Parts of the listing**:

- **Photos**: up to 50, at least 800 pixels wide. The first is the cover. Add a caption and room to each, and move them into order.
- **The place**, **Rooms and beds** (bedroom and bed counts come from here), **Amenities**, **Title and description** and its longer sections.
- **Address**, **Arriving** (how guests get in, check-in and check-out times), **House rules** (pets, smoking, events, children, quiet hours, other rules).
- **Booking rules**: instant book, shortest and longest stay, advance notice, how far ahead guests can book, preparation time, cancellation policy.
- **Prices and fees**: who sets the nightly price, usual and weekend nightly price, cleaning fee, security deposit, extra guest fee, and the **lowest and highest nightly price**. A price outside those limits never reaches the channels.
- **Licence**: a licence or registration number, where the law needs one.
- **Channels**. See [Channels](/guides/channels/).

Photos, descriptions and amenities don't go to the channels automatically. Use the copy buttons to paste them into each channel. Prices, stay rules and free nights do go to the channels once V-01 runs them.

**Tags** under the name are your own labels, such as an owner's name or "Sea view". Guests never see them; the Calendar can filter by them.

## Custom fields

Custom fields hold the details your team needs for each home. Every workspace starts with **Top lock code**, **Bottom lock code**, **Wi-Fi name**, **Wi-Fi password** and **Check-in instructions**.

Each field is short text, long text, a link, or a secret. A secret, such as a lock code, is locked away and shown only to admins, operations and guest comms.

- Anyone who can edit homes fills them in, under **Custom fields** on each home's page.
- An admin adds, renames or removes fields with **Manage fields**. A field appears on every home.
- **Guests see it** puts a field on the guest's [stay page](/guides/guest-stay-page/).
- **Field team sees it** shows it to cleaners and maintenance inside their job.

## Settings

The **Settings** part at the end of the page shows the short code, area, who runs the channels, who sets prices, and when the home was added. Here you also set:

- **Owner**
- **Usual cleaner** and **Usual maintenance person**
- **Damage deposit**. See [Payments and deposits](/guides/payments-and-deposits/).
- **Booking website**. See [Booking website](/guides/booking-website/).

## Archiving

**Archive this property** takes the home off every screen and stops anything more being sent for it. Its bookings and history are kept. To bring an archived home back, ask V-01 support.
