title: Calendar
description: Every home by night, with bookings, blocks, prices and stay rules, and how to change them.
---

The **Calendar** shows every home as a row and every night as a box. It runs from a year back to two years ahead as one long timeline.

## Reading it

- Each night shows its price. A minimum stay or closed date set for that night shows in the box; the home's usual minimum stay is under its name.
- A booking is a bar from the middle of the arrival day to the middle of the departure day, so a turnover shows as two bars meeting. Each bar starts with the mark of the channel it came from: Airbnb, Booking.com, "V" for Vrbo, "E" for Expedia, or a person for a direct booking.
- A bar outlined in red overlaps another booking. It holds no nights and needs sorting out with the guest or the channel.
- Under each home's name is a small **clean or dirty** switch. A check-out makes the home dirty by itself; one tap marks it clean. Dirty is amber, and red when a guest arrives that day.
- **Key** explains every mark.

## Moving around

Scroll sideways with a trackpad, the scrollbar, or Shift and the mouse wheel; swipe on a phone. The arrows move a week at a time, **Today** comes back to today, and the dates beside the arrows let you jump to any date.

## Finding homes

- The search at the top of the names finds a home by name, short code, area or tag.
- **Availability** shows only the homes free for a stay and with room for the party, for example 23 to 25 October for 4 guests. Use it instead of checking home by home.
- **Area**, **Tags**, **Sync**, **Bedrooms** and **Cleaning** narrow the list. **Clear all** removes them.

Click the box with a home's name to open its page.

## Changing nights

Drag across nights, and down across homes, to select them. On a phone, tap a night and stretch it in the panel; on a keyboard, use Shift and the arrows. A panel opens with:

- **Set a price**, **Set a minimum stay**, **Back to the usual price and stay**
- **Block the nights**, **Owner staying**, **Open the blocked nights**
- **Close to arrivals or departures**

Choose one, fill it in, and the button says what will happen, for example "Block 2 nights". You can apply it only to some days of the week.

Things to know:

- Blocking takes only the free nights. Booked nights stay as they are.
- A price must be within the home's lowest and highest price.
- Homes priced by PriceLabs keep PriceLabs' prices; you can still change stay rules.
- Nights that have passed can't be changed.
- For homes still run by your previous system, changes stay in ViVi OS and don't reach the channels.

If your role can't change the calendar, you see it read-only.

## A booking on the calendar

Click a booking to see the guest, stay, total and notes, with **Open reservation**, **Edit** and **Message guest**. See [Reservations](/guides/reservations/).
