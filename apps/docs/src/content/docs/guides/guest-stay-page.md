---
title: Guest stay page
description: The private page each guest gets for their stay, what it shows, and when codes appear.
---

Every confirmed booking has its own private page for the guest. The guest opens it from a link and doesn't sign in. It is one column with large type, in your name and colours.

In ViVi OS this information lives on one page that stays up to date: if you change the Wi-Fi password, the page shows the new one.

## What the guest sees

- the home's photo and title
- the dates, **Check in from** and **Check out by**
- how to get in, and the door code at the right time
- the address, with **Open in Maps**
- the Wi-Fi network and password
- the house rules
- anything else you marked for guests
- **Need anything?**: the phone number guests call and your email

The phone number is set by an admin on the Workspace page, under **Phone guests call**.

## Which details guests see

Each [custom field](/guides/properties/#custom-fields) has a **Guests see it** setting. In the standard set it is on for Top lock code, Wi-Fi name, Wi-Fi password and Check-in instructions, and off for Bottom lock code.

Secret details, such as lock codes and the Wi-Fi password, show only from the day of arrival to the day of departure. A link passed around never gives out codes for months. If ViVi OS makes the door codes on a smart lock, the stay's own code shows once the lock holds it. See [Door locks](/guides/door-locks/).

A cancelled booking's page shows nothing.

## The damage deposit

If the home asks guests for a deposit, the page has a **Damage deposit** part. From 3 days before arrival the guest can press **Add your card**. Stripe's page takes the card, and the guest comes back to see the deposit held. The card is held, not charged.

If the home is set to hold the door code until the deposit is in place, the door code and Wi-Fi password stay hidden until it is held. See [Payments and deposits](/guides/payments-and-deposits/).

## Sending the link

- On a booking's page, **Copy link**, or **See what the guest sees** to check it first.
- The automatic messages include the link.
- In the Inbox, **Stay link** puts it in your reply.
- A guest who books on your [booking website](/guides/booking-website/) is sent to their page when they finish paying.

Send the link only to the guest.
